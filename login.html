Terms & Conditions
  1. No CFA will be provided to non-residential segments of consumers (including government segment, commercial and industrial segment etc.).
  2. The GHS/RWA connection eligible under the scheme shall be dedicated only for common facilities and should not be utilized for providing electricity supply to residential consumers within the GHS/RWA.
  3. The solar plant should meet the technical specification. (Eg. Use of non-DCR modules in any form in the installation shall make the installation ineligible for Central Financial Assistance.)
  4. Pre-existing RTS – (1) Increased installation size shall be eligible for additional CFA under the current scheme only for the balance capacity up to 3 kW of overall RTS plant size. (Eg. Any customer with 1 KW Solar plant, increasing plant size to 3 KW shall be eligible for CFA for difference 2 KW only and thereof as per applicable scheme.)
    (2) Already installed rooftop solar is shifted/relocated to a new location, such a system shall not be eligible for CFA under the scheme.
  5. Registered consumer of DISCOM getting Solar Plant installed will only be eligible for CFA.
  6. Consumer should have an active bank account for availing CFA.
  7. The Solar Plant request has to be processed and registered through MNRE National Portal only. https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/#/
  8. Consumer apply for Govt Base Incentive (GBI) on this link- https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/#/
  9. 1kW of solar plant installation requires 10 sqm (approx. 100sq feet) of area.
